Sample Certification Milestone Guide (v2)

The State must submit Operational Readiness Review (ORR) artifacts at least 60 calendar days before the planned go-live date. The Independent Verification and Validation (IV&V) vendor must deliver a progress report every 30 days. The State must retain certification artifacts for 6 years. Electronic Visit Verification (EVV) criteria are added to the program integrity checklist and must be evidenced in the ORR submission.
